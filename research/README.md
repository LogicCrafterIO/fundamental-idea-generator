### How these were selected

Share price must be under **$0** -- a tradeability rule, so a position fits the ~$4,500 capital unit. Loss-making companies and names with no usable option chain are **not** filtered: those facts come from free endpoints, where a bad response is indistinguishable from a genuine absence, and structuring is optional downstream anyway. They are recorded as caveats on the note instead (`--strict` restores the old filters).

Regenerated by any `fig generate` batch, or rebuilt from the notes on disk with `fig index`. Every note is re-linted as it is indexed, so the scores here reflect the current linter rather than the state at generation time.
