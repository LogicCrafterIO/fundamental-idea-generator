Ticker: ORCL

Description
Formerly Databases and SaaS, Oracle is now more focused on Infrastructure as a Service, which of course includes data centers. 

Reasons
1) It's unclear if the valuation is pricing in a declining enterprise Saas, because it's not priced like Google, which is trading at a Forward PE of 25 while Oracle is at a Forward PE of 17. 
2) The market is overpricing the effects of GPU depreciation and debt servicing
3) AI revenues are likely to increase beyond what is currently in the RPO
4) If Agentic explodes, ORCL has the infra and the software and the data to compete for enterprise, beating out in terms of its strategic advantages as a business, the likes of SNOW [which has the software and data but not the infra and is trading at 20x PS compared to oracle at about 7x PS]. 
5) ORCL has the legislation [known as a legal company pretending to be a software company] and the customer relations necessary to continue charging and this could skyrocket revenue estimates. No other software company, which has relied on software excellence, really has this advantage.
